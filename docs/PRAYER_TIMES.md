# Prayer times and your masjid

Masjid search and selectable prayer timetables are provided by [royyaa.com](https://royyaa.com).

1. Open **Settings → Your masjid** in the launcher.
2. Search by name/city, or enter your masjid's Masjidal ID.
3. Press **Search** on the keyboard or use the matching search button.
4. Check the result's name, address and timezone, then select **Use [masjid name]**.

Name/city search includes masjids already added to Royyaa. If there are no matches, use a Masjidal ID. The example `z0AWYBKj` is Scarborough Islamic Centre in England and is not selected automatically.

Times follow the selected masjid's timezone. Prayer begins and Iqamah are shown separately; Friday can have multiple sessions. Missing times are not guessed. Saved timetables are marked offline after a failed refresh, and expired days are not presented as today's times.

No masjid is preselected. Settings opens automatically on first use; the clock uses device time and prayer times stay unavailable until you choose a masjid. **Clear selection** returns to this state without changing your language or theme. Previously saved Royyaa selections are retained after upgrades; unsupported older selections require choosing again.

Timetables say **Prayer times provided by Royyaa** beneath the prayer cards. Hijri dates are estimates.
